Usage: SysProcOverview <command> [arguments]

Commands:
  pinfo <pid>    Display detailed information about a specific process.
                 If the output display the child process name. It mean
                 the given pid itself is child process.

  sysbrief       Display a brief summary of system resource usage.

Examples:
  SysProcOverview pinfo 1234
  SysProcOverview sysbrief
